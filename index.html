Privacy Policy — Noble Carat

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Noble Carat & Co. ("we", "our", "the shop") operates the Noble Carat mobile app ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from customers. There is no sign-up, no login, no forms, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking.

2. What the app does access

  • Internet access — to fetch live gold and silver reference rates from public data feeds and to sync pricing configuration from our backend (Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India).
  • Notifications (optional) — if you allow them, the app schedules local reminders on your own device (market open/close alerts). These never leave your phone.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, share, or disclose any information to third parties — because there is none to share.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising SDKs that transmit device identifiers.
  • The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

4. Rate disclaimers

Rates shown in the app are indicative base metal reference prices only. They exclude GST, making charges, wastage, and any other charges. The final price is confirmed in store on your invoice. The app is for jewellery retail rate reference only and is not investment advice. The app is not affiliated with IBJA or any rate provider.

5. Data retention

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete.

6. Your rights (India DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have rights over your personal data — including access, correction, and erasure. Since the app processes no personal data, there is nothing to access or erase. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated and you will be notified in the app.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page and linked inside the app.
